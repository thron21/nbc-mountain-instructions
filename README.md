# Mountain base – interactive building instructions

Nightmare Before Christmas winter mountain base, Sections A and B: https://thron21.github.io/nbc-mountain-instructions/

* Tap a row when the piece is on. The next piece glows yellow in the picture, and ticks are saved in the browser.
* Tap **3D** on any picture to turn the model with your finger.
* Tap **Step by step** to see one sub-step at a time. Swipe the picture or tap the side thumbnails to move on.
  It opens again at the last step you looked at.

Made with the **MOC instruction kit**, which you can reuse for future builds:
https://github.com/thron21/moc-instruction-kit
